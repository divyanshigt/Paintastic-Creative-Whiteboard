YOUR ART. YOUR DEVICE.

Privacy & your data

Last updated: 17 October 2026. This describes the Paintastic whiteboard application included in this release.

Your drawings stay on your device

Paintastic processes drawing and text in your browser. The app does not upload your artwork, title or typed text to a server. It does not ask for an account and contains no advertising or analytics code.

What is stored locally

  • The latest board image, title and background settings are saved in this browser’s local storage when available.
  • The service worker caches the app’s own pages, scripts, icons and coloring pages to support offline use after successful setup.
  • Undo history is kept in memory and is lost when you close or reload the page.
  • Export creates a PNG file in the download location managed by your browser or operating system.

Control and deletion

You can remove saved artwork and cached app files using your browser’s site-data settings for this website. This is separate from the Clear artwork button: Clear resets your canvas and autosaves the blank board, but can still be undone while the app remains open. Clearing browser data removes recovery data and may remove offline access until your next online visit.

Downloaded PNG files remain on your device until you delete them. Browser storage may be cleared by the browser or operating system, and is not a backup. Export important work.

Hosting and app distribution

Loading the website connects to its hosting provider. That provider may process technical request data such as IP address, browser details and requested URLs under its own policies. The browser, operating system and any app store used to install Paintastic may also process information under their respective policies. These services operate separately from Paintastic’s drawing code.

Device access

This release does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts access. It uses mouse, touch, pen and keyboard input for creating artwork. Installation and downloads are controlled by the browser or operating system.

Updates

New app versions may update this notice. Offline copies can show the notice from the cached app version until an update is activated.
